# Library backgrounds

The server library and server workspaces share a full-page background for each theme. The artwork was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and encoded as WebP. The filenames in `assets/library-backgrounds` match the theme IDs. A subtly blurred, translucent layer takes its tint from the active theme. Scenery stays in place during navigation while the glass changes shape and workspace controls move along curved paths between the selected card and their positions. The moving server tint fades into the scenery at its edges. The scenery shifts slightly with the mouse outside the transition. Reduced-motion mode keeps the scenery still and switches views immediately.

## Normal

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one full-bleed landscape background for a desktop server library, cinematic naturalistic environment concept art with restrained contrast and polished depth. Compose for a wide horizontal crop through the center of the scene. Keep first third dark and quiet for overlaid controls, and the principal scene in the middle-right and central vertical half. No lettering, interface, logo, standalone badge, border or watermark. A blue-hour mountain town in a steep river valley, seen from a high rocky bank; blue slate roofs and a stone bridge lead to a handful of warmly lit windows. Dark navy and steel-blue shadows, subdued cyan river reflections, understated and slightly more modern than the other themes. The settlement and bridge sit on the right; left is a quiet dark bank.

## Minecraft

Use case: stylized-concept. Scene rendered like a beautiful high-end in-game screenshot with convincing Minecraft terrain, carefully textured blocks at landscape scale, natural atmospheric perspective, subtle fog, and deep olive-green shadows drawn from the theme's grass-block icon. Interesting close and distant layers. On the left third, quiet dark forest shade and riverbank with minimal detail for overlaid interface buttons, village and warm highlights in the middle-right. Preserve recognizable detail in the vertical central half for cropping. No text, UI, logos, freestanding grass-block emblems, watermark, or pasted cutouts. Output a single full-bleed image.
